# Research log

Method (borrowed from REA's evidence discipline): every finding lists its source, what was observed vs. assumed, and what's unknown. Rekeep studies Paste only through public material and visible behaviour — never its binaries.

## Paste (pasteapp.io) — observed from public pages, 15 Oct 2026

| Finding | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Shelf is a full-width band at the bottom of the screen; strip above cards holds search, a "Clipboard" pill, pinboard chips with coloured dots, "+", "…" | product imagery on pasteapp.io | Observed |
| Cards have a coloured header band (type, relative time, app icon); colour comes from the source app | product imagery | Observed (colour rule inferred) |
| Selection is a blue outline around the card | product imagery | Observed |
| Features: unlimited history, pinboards, paste stack (sequence), Shortcuts automation, iCloud sync, ignore rules for passwords/apps, Apple Intelligence suggestions, MCP/AI tools, teams | pasteapp.io + /use-cases | Claimed by Paste, not verified |
| Pricing: 7-day trial, "See plans"; no prices on the home page | pasteapp.io | Observed |

**Where Rekeep deliberately differs:** neutral cards (no per-app colour bands — a stable reading surface), a floating tray separated from the screen edges and Dock, Compose as a first-class workflow, honest permission states.

## Platform facts — verified in the SDK (Xcode 27, macOS 27 / iOS 27)

| Finding | Source |
|---|---|

| Reading `types`/`changeCount` does not trigger the alert; reading content does | header docs + self-test (no alert on private pasteboards) |
| `glassEffect(_:in:)`, `GlassEffectContainer`, `.glass` button styles available | `SwiftUICore.swiftinterface` |
