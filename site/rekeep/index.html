Rekeep is a quiet, private home for everything worth copying — text, links, images, colours, code, and files — on your Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

On your Mac, press ⇧⌘V and your recent clips rise from the bottom of the screen as clear cards. Pick one; it’s pasted where you were working. Type to search everything you’ve kept, including words inside images.

On iPhone and iPad, save with one tap or from the Share sheet, and type any saved clip with the Rekeep keyboard.

Private by design. Your clips stay on your device. No account, no analytics, no servers.
