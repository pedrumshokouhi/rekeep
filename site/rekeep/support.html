Rekeep isn’t saving what I copy on my Mac

macOS asks before any app reads the clipboard. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Paste from Other Apps and set Rekeep to Always Allow. Also check that capture isn’t paused (menu bar icon › Resume Capture) and that the app you copied from isn’t excluded (Settings › Privacy).

How do I paste a clip on my Mac?

Press ⇧⌘V, pick a clip with Return or a click, then press ⌘V. The shelf never takes focus from the app you were using, so the clip lands right where your cursor was. Rekeep doesn’t type into other apps, so it needs no Accessibility permission.

How do I save things on iPhone?

iOS doesn’t let any app watch the clipboard in the background, so Rekeep saves at the moments iOS allows: switch to the Rekeep keyboard and whatever you last copied is saved and shown first; open Rekeep and tap Paste; or use the Share button in any app and choose Rekeep. To stop iOS asking each time, set Settings › Apps › Rekeep › Paste from Other Apps to Allow.

Setting up the Rekeep keyboard

Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard › Rekeep, then tap Rekeep and turn on Allow Full Access so it can show your saved clips. Switch to it with the globe key. It inserts text clips; for images and files, copy them in the app.

Shortcuts

  • Mac: ⇧⌘V opens the shelf (change it in Settings). Arrow keys move, Return pastes, ⇧Return pastes plain text, Space previews, ⌘1–9 pastes one of the first nine clips, Tab switches collections, Esc closes.
  • Paste Queue: add clips with ⌘K, then press ⌥⌘V anywhere to paste the next one.
  • Shortcuts app: Save Text to Rekeep, Copy Latest Clip, Copy Clip, Search Rekeep, Get Recent Clips.

What does Rekeep cost?

Nothing. Every feature is free, with no ads, no subscription, and no account.

Do my clips sync between devices?

Not in version 1.0 — each device keeps its own library, privately. On Mac you can export your library to a folder and import it on another Mac.
